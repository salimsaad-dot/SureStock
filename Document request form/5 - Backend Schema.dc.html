Backend Schema

PostgreSQL tables, fields, relationships, and the rules that keep the numbers honest.

1. Conventions

  • Primary keys are UUID v7 — time-ordered, so they index well and can be generated on an offline client.
  • Every table carries created_at and updated_at as timestamptz, stored in UTC.
  • Money is numeric(12,2); quantities are numeric(12,3) so goods sold by weight work without a schema change.
  • Deletion is soft (archived_at) for master data and forbidden for transactions.
  • Every table carries location_id from day one, even with one shop, so a second branch needs no migration.
  • Enumerations are Postgres enum types, listed in section 6.

2. Entity relationships

location 1─* user 1─* till_shift 1─* sale category 1─* product 1─* product_variant 1─* stock_movement supplier 1─* purchase_order 1─* purchase_order_line sale 1─* sale_line sale 1─* payment sale 1─0..1 sale (refund_of) stock_take 1─* stock_take_line product_variant 1─* price_history customer 1─* sale

The central rule: stock_movement is append-only and is the only thing that changes stock. product_variant.quantity_on_hand is a cached running total maintained inside the same transaction, and a nightly job asserts it equals the sum of movements. If they ever disagree, the movements win.

payment

One row per tender: sale_id, method (cash, mobile_money, card, account, change), amount, reference (mobile money transaction ID), provider, and status. Split payments are simply several rows. A constraint asserts that the sum of payments equals the sale total.

5. Indexes and integrity

  • GIN trigram on product.name and product_variant.sku for typo-tolerant search.
  • B-tree on product_variant.barcode, sale.sold_at, sale.till_shift_id, and a composite on (variant_id, occurred_at DESC) for movements.
  • Partial index on product_variant where quantity_on_hand <= reorder_point to make the low-stock list instant.
  • Constraints: sale totals equal the sum of their lines; payments equal the sale total; counted quantities are non-negative; a variant's selling price cannot be below zero.
  • A nightly job reconciles quantity_on_hand against the movement ledger and raises an alert on any drift.
