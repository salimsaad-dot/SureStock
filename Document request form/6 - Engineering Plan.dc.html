Engineering Plan

Build order, dependencies, and acceptance criteria, task by task.

1. How this plan is organised

Seven phases, each ending in something demonstrable. A task is only finished when its acceptance criteria pass and its tests are in the suite. Nothing in a later phase begins before its listed dependencies are done.

Sizes are relative: S is up to a day, M two to three days, L about a week for one developer. Total is roughly ten to twelve weeks solo, six to seven with two developers working in parallel from Phase 2.

Definition of done, applied to every task: code reviewed and merged; unit tests for the logic and an integration test for the endpoint; role permissions verified; loading, empty, and error states implemented; works at phone and counter widths; keyboard navigable; no new Sentry errors after a day in staging.

9. Critical path and parallel work

T-01 → T-02 → T-03 → T-06 → T-07 → T-14 → T-16 → T-22 → T-32 → T-33

Everything else can be scheduled around that spine. With two developers, one takes the sell path (T-14 to T-20) while the other takes inventory and purchasing (T-10 to T-13, T-27, T-28). Design work on the sell and payment screens must land before T-14 starts; reports design can wait until Phase 5.

Earliest useful milestone: after T-16 the shop can legitimately sell on the system with pen-and-paper fallback. Consider a limited soft launch there — real transactions will surface problems no test does.
